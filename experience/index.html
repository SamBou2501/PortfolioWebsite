Lead Game Designer

PLAYA3ULL GAMES · 2022 – Present

I am the Lead Designer and “Vision Holder” for multiple projects.

As Lead Designer

  • Designing gameplay systems (loops that help engage players).
  • Designing game mechanics that are “flavourful” AND that feel good to play.
  • (Generally) creating the narrative and making sure it fits within gameplay.
  • Making sure the narrative is conveyed to players through gameplay.
  • Designing and balancing said systems and mechanics.
  • Running playtests of my designs, gathering feedback from players, and working on that feedback.
  • Relaying between different teams and adjusting designs as needed to make sure the designs are fulfilled while not putting too much strain on a single team.
  • Helping devs improve “gameplay feel” — especially around Sound, Player Experience, and UI.

As Vision Holder

  • Making sure said systems and mechanics are implemented correctly in each game.
  • Creating the visual direction for a game.
  • Making sure visual direction, narrative, and gameplay all align.

While at PLAYA3ULL GAMES so far, I have seen two games brought to “gold release” (full release with more updates/expansions to come), with another two brought to beta and alpha stages, awaiting their move to the next stage.
