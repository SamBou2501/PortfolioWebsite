Making ZORDE's map

Level design · zombie horde shooter

I created a huge medieval castle map with paranatural and eldritch elements, as it was a castle overtaken by otherworldly undead who players had to take back, fortify, and defend against waves of undead. It was created as part of the Third Person Zombie Horde Shooter Roguelite (dubbed 'ZORDE') I was making at the time.

The level went through many iterations and improvements, but it still wasn't close to done, mostly because level design isn't my strongest skill.

However, I'm incredibly proud of the research and time that went into visualising and realising not only the map and level design but the game as a whole.
