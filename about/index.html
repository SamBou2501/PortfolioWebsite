Introduction / TLDR

Hello, I am Samuel! I am a Game Developer and Systems designer who has been working in games for a few years. I am a HUGE gamer, who started very young and never 'grew out of it'. I love being able to create my own worlds and immerse myself them.

Read below for more details.

My Love of Gaming

I have always been a huge gamer — basically ever since we first got a Game Boy when I was about 7 or 8. From there it went to Nintendo DS's, then World of Warcraft, then StarCraft and Starcraft 2 and League of Legends.

When SC2 came out I fell in love with the Zerg and the sci-fi world all over again. To this day still my absolutely most loved game. Howevre I also put countless hours into mastering characters in LoL and leveling up character in WoW.

As I got older, towards the end of my schooling years, I decided to branch out more as SC2 unfortunately began to die, and WoW just wasn't holding my attention anymore. 2020 was when I actually first made my Steam account and got a game on there!

Dead by Daylight was the game I sunk my teeth into during covid. From there and over the last few years I was able to find and play even more games I loved as I expanded my repertoire even further — Skyrim, Vermintide, Control, Destiny 2, Darktide, Elden Ring, Outriders, Grim Dawn, Helldivers 2, WWZ: Aftermath and Hades (1 and now recently 2).

Note, this list doesn't include non-Steam games that I absolutely loved, including Spellbreak (RIP), Fortnite, Evil Dead: The Game, MTG Arena and Legends of Runeterra (also RIP).

Getting Into Game Design

I have always been incredibly creative and so the idea of creating my own worlds and mechanics and improving things to make them better was always my dream.

StarCraft 2 is where I first got my taste of game design and developed through their awesome editor! I made many games from it, and began designing my own games, getting people to playtest them, and taking that feedback onboard to iterate and improve said game.

Then during covid I dabbled in it again by making a ginormous document for how to "fix" a game I really liked and played (a LOT) at the time, due to their very questionable balance and design decisions driving me mad. I was also all over forums for games I was playing and spinning up new ideas for content, balance, you name it.

Then when the opportunity came to actually start being part of making games at the family company we started — PLAYA3ULL GAMES — I immediately jumped on it knowing I was incredibly lucky! And from there I realised this is what I'd love to do all the time, and quickly decided to study it properly.
