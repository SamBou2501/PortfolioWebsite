Making ZORDE's map

Level design · zombie horde shooter

For another assessment, I created a huge medieval castle map with paranatural and eldritch elements, as part of the zombie horde shooter (dubbed ZORDE) I was making at the time.

The level went through many iterations and improvements, but it still wasn't close to done, mostly because level design isn't my strongest skill.

However, I'm incredibly proud of the research and time that went into visualising and realising not only the map and level design but the game as a whole.
