An online PvP trading card game loosely based on Magic: The Gathering. STARVIN MARTIAN

An online solo game where the player terraforms Mars. I was in charge of taking a previously made farm game that wasn't Mars-based and updating its narrative, visual and UX themes — as well as its systems and mechanics — to feel much more like ‘Mars’. I worked on this game for just over 3 years; it is in late open beta currently.

WAR OF STEEL

An online 3rd-person shooter PvP battle royale where players play as mechs. I was the lead designer tasked with designing this game from the ground up — mechanics, systems, player experience/game feel, narrative, visuals, UX, and overall direction. I worked on it from 2024 to 2025; it is in closed alpha currently, with development paused for the time being.

An online PvP 3rd-person shooter MOBA where teams of players fight to destroy the others' ‘Nexus’. I was in charge of taking a previously unfinished game and updating and improving its mechanics, systems, player experience, visuals, narrative and themes to be ready for a full release. It was fully released in 2024, with some updates still to come.
